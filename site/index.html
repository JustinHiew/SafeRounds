Round 01 · Overview

Safety rounds for your patient-facing AI.

SafeRounds reads the conversations your AI assistant, coaches and support staff have with patients. It checks every reply against your own clinical protocol and sends anything risky to a person, with the exact words that raised the flag.

Round 02 · What it checks

Five checks on every staff reply, judged against your protocol.

Each clinic brings its own protocol, reference facts and patient-record fields. The checks read those, so the judge holds your replies to your rules, not to a generic idea of "safe".

Safety

Advice that could cause harm if the patient followed it, such as changing a dose without the prescriber or dismissing a warning symptom.

Escalation

A red flag the reply doesn't send to the right place, plainly and in that reply: emergency, same-day or routine, as your protocol defines them.

Accuracy

Anything that contradicts your reference sheet, including your own phone numbers and service hours.

Scope

Staff working beyond their role: support agents giving clinical advice, AI assistants diagnosing, coaches changing treatment.

Grounding

Replies that contradict or ignore the patient's record, or invent details about them.

Round 03 · How it works

From transcript to reviewer in three steps.

  1. Send conversations

    Through the API, or as a CSV or helpdesk export. Names, phone numbers, emails, Medicare numbers and addresses are removed before anything is stored or judged. Your clinic's own numbers stay, so the judge can check them.

  2. Every reply is judged

    Claude reads the conversation with your protocol and the patient record, and quotes the exact words behind every verdict. A quote that isn't in the conversation or record, or any answer it can't check, goes to a person. Nothing passes by default.

  3. People make the call

    Reviewers confirm or overturn each flag in a review queue. Their corrections become test cases, monitoring alerts you when rates drift, and a regression gate blocks prompt or model changes that catch fewer high-severity problems.

Round 05 · Health data

Built for patient conversations from the start.

  • De-identified first. Personal details are removed before a conversation is stored or sent to a model.
  • Australian hosting. Deployment in Sydney is being set up now.
  • Every read is logged. Viewing a conversation writes an audit record. Logs and alerts carry numbers and ids, never conversation text.
  • Each clinic sees only its own data. Access is scoped per clinic on every request.
  • Quality assurance, not clinical advice. SafeRounds triages conversations for human review. It doesn't diagnose or treat.
Round 06 · Built with

Claude does the reading. People keep the final say.

The judge runs on Claude through the Anthropic API, with prompt caching for each clinic's protocol and the batch API for large audits. It writes its evidence and reasoning before its verdict, and a spend limit stops it before it can overrun its budget.
