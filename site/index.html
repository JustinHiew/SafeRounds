    SafeRounds reads the conversations your AI assistant, peer workers and support staff have with patients. It judges each reply against your protocol and the patient's record, underlines the words behind every verdict, and sends anything it can't clear to a person. It is quality assurance for patient-facing chat, not clinical advice.

    02 · Five checks

    Five checks on every staff reply, judged against your rules.

    Safety

    Advice that could cause harm if followed: a dose change without the prescriber, a warning sign played down, a distressed patient left to cope alone.

    Escalation

    A red flag that isn't sent to the right tier, plainly and in the same reply: emergency (000), urgent same day, or routine review, as your protocol defines them.

    Accuracy

    Anything that contradicts your reference sheet, including your own phone numbers and opening hours.

    Scope

    Staff working beyond their role: a support agent giving clinical advice, an assistant diagnosing, a peer worker advising on medicine.

    Grounding

    Replies that contradict or ignore the patient's record, or invent details about them.

    Each service brings its own protocol, reference facts and record fields. The judge reads those, so a peer-support service and a weight-management clinic are held to different rules.

    03 · How it works

    From transcript to reviewer.

    1

    Send

    Through the API, or as a CSV or helpdesk export. Names, phone numbers, emails, Medicare numbers and addresses are removed before anything is stored or judged. Your service's own numbers are kept so they can be checked.

    2

    Judge

    Claude reads each conversation with your protocol and the patient's record, writes its evidence before its verdict, and quotes the exact words. A quote that isn't in the transcript, or an answer it can't give, goes to a person.

    3

    Review

    Reviewers confirm or overturn each flag in a queue. Overturned verdicts become test cases for your service.

    Once a service is running, monitoring alerts you when flag rates drift, and a regression gate blocks any prompt or model change that catches fewer high-severity problems.

    05 · Patient data

    Built for patient conversations from the start.

    • De-identified firstPersonal details are removed before a conversation is stored or sent to a model.
    • Australian hostingThe service and its database are being set up in Sydney. Judging calls go to Anthropic's API.
    • Every read is loggedViewing a conversation writes an audit record. Logs carry ids and counts, never conversation text.
    • One service, one tenancyAccess is scoped to each service on every request.
    • What it isn'tNot clinical advice, not clinical decision support, and not a crisis service. It triages conversations for human review.

    06 · Built on

    The judge runs on Claude through the Anthropic API, with prompt caching for each service's protocol, the batch API for audits, a hard spend limit, and evidence written before every verdict. People keep the final say.
