How pilot data is handled

This page describes what happens today, during free pilot audits run directly with each design partner, and what this website itself collects. It is not a description of a future hosted service.

Last updated 11 October 2026

Please don't email patient conversations. Before any data is shared we agree in writing the scope of the audit, how transcripts are de-identified, and a secure way to transfer them.

1. Before anything is shared

  • You de-identify transcripts before sending them: names, contact details, dates of birth, addresses, Medicare and other record numbers, and anything else that could identify a person.
  • We agree the number of conversations, the time period they cover, the protocol they are checked against, and when the audit ends.
  • We agree a transfer method. Email is not one of them.
  • We provide a one-page data-flow summary, complete your privacy or security questionnaire, and sign your data-handling agreement if you have one.

2. The automatic de-identification pass and its limits

SafeRounds runs its own de-identification pass on every conversation before it is stored or sent for review. It replaces common identifier patterns such as phone numbers, email addresses, dates and record numbers.

It is pattern-based and can miss things, in particular:

  • names, nicknames and family members mentioned in free text;
  • unusual identifiers, misspellings and numbers written out in words;
  • details that identify someone only in combination, such as a rare condition plus a suburb plus an occupation.

It is a second safety net, not a replacement for your own de-identification. SafeRounds will not attempt to re-identify anyone in the data you share.

3. What is sent to Anthropic

The review is done by Claude, a model made by Anthropic, through Anthropic's commercial API, over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. For each conversation the following is sent:

  • the de-identified conversation text;
  • the relevant parts of your clinical protocol;
  • any de-identified record text you supply for context.

Anthropic may process this data outside Australia. Under Anthropic's commercial terms, API inputs and outputs are not used to train its models. Anthropic's published policy (checked 11 October 2026) is to delete API inputs and outputs within 30 days, except where content is flagged by its safety systems, in which case it may be kept for up to 2 years. See Anthropic's retention policy and commercial terms.

4. What SafeRounds keeps, and for how long

  • Kept: the de-identified conversations, the review results with their quoted evidence, reviewer decisions, and an audit record of who opened each conversation and when.
  • Not kept: anything you have not sent for the agreed audit. Original, identified transcripts are not requested.
  • How long: only for the agreed audit. Everything is deleted at the end of the audit, or earlier whenever you ask.
  • Where: during pilots, data is kept on a single computer controlled by the founder, with full-disk encryption, and is not copied to cloud storage. The exact arrangement is agreed with you in writing before the audit starts. Hosting in Sydney is planned before any ongoing service and is not in place yet.

5. Who can see it

  • Only the founder, Justin Hiew, and the people you nominate from your own organisation.
  • If your reviewers use the review queue, each has their own account, and accounts are removed when the audit ends. Otherwise, flags reach you in the written report.
  • Opening a conversation in the review queue is recorded in an audit log for everyone, the founder included. You can ask to see it.
  • Your data is not shared with anyone else, used for marketing, or used to build public examples. The examples on this website are synthetic.

6. If something goes wrong

If pilot data is lost or accessed without authorisation, or this is suspected, you will be told within 72 hours of SafeRounds becoming aware of it, with what was affected and what has been done, so you can meet your own obligations under the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme.

7. Deletion

Email justin@saferounds.dev at any time to have your data deleted. Deletion covers the stored conversations, results and reviewer notes, and is confirmed in writing. The audit log records the deletion itself. Copies held by Anthropic expire on Anthropic's schedule (section 3).

8. Privacy law

SafeRounds handles pilot data in line with the Australian Privacy Principles, whether or not the Privacy Act 1988 applies to it as a small business.

9. This website

saferounds.dev sets no cookies and runs no analytics or tracking scripts. It is served by Cloudflare, and it loads fonts from Google Fonts and one animation library from cdnjs. Those providers receive your IP address to deliver the files, as with any website.

10. What SafeRounds is not

SafeRounds is a retrospective review and quality-assurance tool. Its intended purpose is quality assurance of past conversations. It does not see conversations as they happen, does not change or block replies, and is not intended to diagnose, treat or inform the care of an individual patient. Clinical responsibility stays with your service.

Questions
