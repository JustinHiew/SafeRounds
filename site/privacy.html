Data handling for pilots

What happens to conversation data during a free pilot audit, and what this website collects. It describes the pilot arrangement today, not a future hosted service.

Last updated 12 October 2026. First published 11 October 2026.

Please don’t email patient data. Before anything is shared, the scope of the audit, how transcripts are de-identified and a secure way to transfer them are agreed in writing.

Before anything is shared

  • You de-identify transcripts before sending them: names, contact details, dates of birth, addresses, Medicare and other record numbers, and anything else that could identify a person.
  • The number of conversations, the period they cover, the protocol they are checked against and the end date of the audit are agreed in advance.
  • A transfer method is agreed in advance. Email is not an option.
  • SafeRounds provides a one-page data-flow summary, completes your privacy or security questionnaire, and signs your data-handling agreement if you have one.

Automatic de-identification, and its limits

SafeRounds runs its own de-identification pass on every conversation before it is stored or checked. It replaces common identifier patterns such as phone numbers, email addresses, dates and record numbers.

It is pattern-based and can miss:

  • Names, nicknames and family members mentioned in free text.
  • Unusual identifiers, misspellings and numbers written out in words.
  • Details that identify someone only in combination, such as a rare condition, a suburb and an occupation together.

It is a second line of defence, not a replacement for your own de-identification. SafeRounds will not attempt to re-identify anyone in the data you share.

What is sent to Anthropic

Each conversation is checked by Claude, a model made by Anthropic, through Anthropic’s commercial API over an encrypted connection. For each conversation SafeRounds sends:

  • The de-identified conversation text.
  • The relevant parts of your clinical protocol.
  • Any de-identified record details you supply for context.

Anthropic may process this data outside Australia. Under Anthropic’s commercial terms, API inputs and outputs are not used to train its models. Anthropic’s published policy (checked 11 October 2026) is to delete API inputs and outputs within 30 days, except where content is flagged by its safety systems, when it may be kept for up to 2 years. See Anthropic’s retention policy and commercial terms.

What SafeRounds keeps, and for how long

  • Kept: the de-identified conversations, the results with their quoted evidence, reviewer decisions, and a record of who opened each conversation and when.
  • Not kept: original, identified transcripts. They are never requested.
  • How long: only for the agreed audit. Everything is deleted at the end of the audit, or sooner on request.
  • Where: on one computer controlled by the founder, with full-disk encryption, and not copied to cloud storage. The exact arrangement is agreed with you in writing before the audit starts. Hosting in Sydney is planned and not yet in place.

Who can see it

  • Only the founder, Justin Hiew, and the people you nominate from your own organisation.
  • If your reviewers use the review queue, each has their own account, and accounts are removed when the audit ends. Otherwise, flags reach you in the written report.
  • Opening a conversation in the review queue is logged for everyone, the founder included. You can ask to see the log.
  • Your data is not shared with anyone else, used for marketing, or used for public examples. The examples on saferounds.dev are synthetic.

If something goes wrong

If pilot data is lost or accessed without authorisation, or this is suspected, you will be told within 72 hours of SafeRounds becoming aware of it, with what was affected and what has been done, so you can meet your own obligations under the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme.

Deletion

Email justin@saferounds.dev at any time to have your data deleted. Deletion covers the stored conversations, results and reviewer notes, and is confirmed in writing. The access log records the deletion itself. Copies held by Anthropic expire on Anthropic’s schedule, described above.

Privacy law

SafeRounds handles pilot data in line with the Australian Privacy Principles, whether or not the Privacy Act 1988 applies to SafeRounds as a small business.

This website

saferounds.dev sets no cookies and runs no analytics or tracking scripts. It is served by Cloudflare and loads fonts from Google Fonts and one animation library from cdnjs. Those providers receive your IP address to deliver the files, as with any website.

What SafeRounds is not

SafeRounds is a retrospective quality-assurance tool. It does not see conversations as they happen, does not change or block replies, and is not intended to diagnose, treat or inform the care of an individual patient. Clinical responsibility stays with your service.

Questions

Email justin@saferounds.dev. If anything here doesn’t fit your organisation’s requirements, raise it before the pilot starts so it can be resolved first.
